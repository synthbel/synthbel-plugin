# Question sensitivity and answers without fear

Use literature, Belarusian survey research and the relevant corpus to give a reasoned model judgment. When the user asks for a fear adjustment, provide a concrete central counterfactual distribution and coherent alternative scenarios. The model selects and explains the adjustment; requiring the user to supply an arbitrary concealment percentage leaves the main task undone. Lack of an observed fear-free reference limits empirical validation, but does not by itself prevent an explicitly labelled model estimate.

When the request is only to review a draft question, give the sensitivity assessment and design consequence without inventing an unrequested numerical result.

## Define the counterfactual and baseline

Record the original question, options, audience, fieldwork and mode. The default counterfactual is the same weighted audience answering the same question at the same time without anticipated adverse consequences of disclosing its view. Preserve its information, genuine opinions and uncertainty. Removing disclosure fear does not mean removing repression or war from people's lives, changing their opinions, or making everyone informed.

For an observed survey, start from the verified full distribution over the eligible respondents. Retain original response categories and code meanings. If the audience is only completed interviews, say so; a population estimate including nonparticipants requires a separate selection model. If a new question has no observed baseline, obtain a separately labelled estimate of recorded answers and keep that uncertainty separate from the F adjustment. Do not alter an A1 output silently.

For routed questions, retain the condition and its denominator. A candidate share among reported voters and a protected estimate among all completers have different bases. Keep unknown-routing unasked mass separate from DK; recovering its opinions requires an explicit additional imputation model.

When participation loss is also requested, use the [participation-scenario procedure](participation-scenarios.md). Give separate missing-group assumptions and conditional mixture results; a dropoff percentage is not an additional percentage-point fear correction. For report-only results, distinguish the overall survey sample size from an unreported item denominator and assess only the published response groups.

## Assemble the evidence

Search original wording and a meaningful reformulation. Retrieve comparable distributions and relevant FactsDB statements, then inspect original questionnaires, methods and report pages. A similar question is a lead; verify actor, action, horizon, population, mode and response scale before borrowing numbers.

Verify administered question and option order from the instrument when order affects interpretation. Numeric codes and dataset column order establish storage order only. Preserve a DK label even if its code lies between agreement and disagreement; possible midpoint effects remain a hypothesis until presentation is verified. For sponsor-perception contrasts, record whether that item preceded the outcomes; when order is unknown or later, retain reverse causality alongside political projection.

In a prediction comparison, apply the frozen source exclusions before retrieving or opening linked result notes. Protected outcomes and their derivatives can occur inside methods assessments and earlier audits. Use the [comparison procedure](protected-comparison.md) when evaluating a correction against an indirect method; ordinary unrestricted research and held-out prediction require different evidence views.

For current investigation, use the connected MCP tools in [tool-contract.md](tool-contract.md). Search original wording and reformulations with `corpus_search`, follow `corpus_history` pagination, use `search_findings`, and verify relevant report pages with `read_report_page` and `page_image`. Use `browse_source` for instruments and methods. With microdata scope, use `crosstab` for the needed joint diagnostics; respondent downloads are unnecessary by default. Preserve the returned release and source locators. An unavailable tool is an access boundary, not corpus absence. A reviewed case is an optional lead, never the scope of research.

Build three connected layers:

1. **Literature:** what mechanisms, directions, experimental results and alternative explanations are established in their own settings? Use evidence quality and contextual relevance, rather than transporting a foreign percentage automatically.
2. **Belarusian research:** what did researchers actually measure, infer or assume? Retrieve methodological assessments and verify the relevant original reports through the MCP tools. A reported scenario is not an observed bias estimate; an indirect method can have its own errors.
3. **This audience and question:** original distributions, explicit refusals, DK, substantive neutral answers, participation records, within-wave associations, repeated items and matched experimental/mode/house/event contrasts. Preserve structural not-asked and unknown missingness separately. No DK option means DK is unmeasured, not zero.

Across surveys, compare three channels separately: answer substitution, item refusal and participation loss. A neutral option can carry a contrast that appears in DK on another answer menu. For participation, preserve invitations, starts, screening, interruptions and completions with their own bases; verify status labels and whether supplied partial records cover all interruptions. Repeated-item event comparisons require the same battery within each series. A flat overall DK mean can conceal large topic-specific changes.

## Make the model judgment

Explain the likely disclosure mechanism, its direction and the strongest competing explanation. The observed sponsor, mode or event gap can inform the scale of a judgment, but is not automatically a fear effect. Apply a subgroup contrast only to its proper audience weight. A stable direction with an unstable magnitude warrants a broader alternative range; a sign-changing component requires its own reasoning rather than inheriting another component's sign.

Select a central correction and explain its preference over zero, full reassignment of DK, or copying a different survey's result; say when the evidence cannot rank these alternatives. Distinguish a topic-based sensitivity prior from evidence that calibrates magnitude or donor categories. Discount contrasts affected by composition, projection, genuine opinion changes, wording or comprehension, and explain any fraction attributed to disclosure fear. A panel or provider difference needs a supported disclosure mechanism before serving as a fear-magnitude reference. Use stronger local evidence to constrain weaker analogies. Precision should match the evidence; elaborate arithmetic does not justify many decimals in the recommendation.

Allocate the proposed changes explicitly between original categories. Keep a genuine uncertainty component; do not force every hesitant person into opposition or assume only opposition can be concealed. A support-to-opposition transfer needs its own reason. Preserve both directions when evidence warrants them. A zero central adjustment is permissible when substantively argued, with meaningful nonzero alternatives if plausible; it is not a default refusal because truth is unobserved.

Use the [counterfactual output contract](fear-output.md) for full-distribution checks and visible labels for judgments, directional uncertainty and selected scenario ranges. Retain observations, evidence, chosen transfers and assumptions separately.

## Check and deliver

Check source fidelity, comparability, original codes/bases, arithmetic, donor feasibility and robustness to plausible alternative mechanisms. Independent review challenges these choices; agreement between models is not validation against hidden beliefs. Do not score the adjustment against the original recorded answers as if agreement established the counterfactual's accuracy.

Lead with the proposed observed-versus-without-fear table and its scope. Explain the few source findings that materially determine the correction, the model's chosen assumptions, and the most consequential alternative. State separately what remains a qualitative sensitivity judgment, what is observed and what is estimated by the model. If core wording, categories or the baseline cannot be recovered, identify that concrete missing input; do not substitute a different estimand.

Where appropriate, propose a clearer direct question, a list experiment, randomized response or another protected procedure, explaining its assumptions. Such a pilot can inform future revisions; it is not a prerequisite for supplying the requested present model judgment.
